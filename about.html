Dr. Saurabh Sudhir Chipde

Senior Consultant Urologist, Robotic & Laparoscopic Surgeon, and Kidney Transplant Specialist at Rajshree Apollo Hospitals, Indore.

Academic Pedigree & Clinical Leadership

Pioneering Advanced Urology with Compassionate Care

Dr. Saurabh Sudhir Chipde is among Central India’s most distinguished and respected urological surgeons. Based full-time at Rajshree Apollo Hospitals Indore, Dr. Chipde provides tertiary-level surgical care for complex prostate disorders, end-stage renal disease requiring kidney transplantation, robotic uro-oncology, and advanced endourology.

His medical foundation was built at India’s premier medical institutions. Dr. Chipde completed his Master of Surgery (MS in General Surgery) at the All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi, followed by his superspecialty MCh in Urology at the Sanjay Gandhi Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences (SGPGIMS), Lucknow.

International Robotic Surgery Fellowship

To bring the highest level of minimally invasive precision to Central India, Dr. Chipde completed specialized clinical fellowship training in Robotic Urological Surgery at the world-renowned Roswell Park Cancer Institute in Buffalo, New York, USA.

Surgical Milestones & Achievements

  • First Robotic Prostate Surgery in Madhya Pradesh: Successfully introduced and performed the landmark first robotic-assisted radical prostatectomy in the state.
  • Pioneer in Water Vapor Therapy: Over 100+ Rezūm™ steam therapy procedures performed in Indore, offering men relief from enlarged prostate without major surgery.
  • Extensive Kidney Transplant Experience: Leading multidisciplinary living-donor renal transplant teams, performing donor nephrectomies and vascular reconstructive surgery.
  • Advanced Endourology & RIRS: High-volume laser surgeon for complex and staghorn renal calculi using flexible ureterorenoscopy (RIRS) and mini-PCNL.

Clinical Philosophy & Patient-Centred Care

"Medicine is fundamentally about listening, transparent counseling, and recommending the least invasive treatment that provides the most durable, high-quality clinical outcome."

Dr. Chipde emphasizes evidence-based clinical protocols. Every patient undergoes an individualized evaluation where the benefits, realistic expectations, and potential risks of both medical and surgical pathways are explained in simple, respectful terms.
