Kidney Transplantation & Renal Surgery

Dr. Chipde brings extensive experience in urology and renal transplantation, with a focus on coordinated, patient-centred transplant care at Rajshree Apollo Hospitals Indore.

Comprehensive Renal Transplant Program

Understanding Kidney Failure & When Transplant is Considered

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) can gradually progress to End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD), where the kidneys lose over 85–90% of their functional capacity. At this stage, waste products and excess fluid accumulate in the bloodstream, requiring either maintenance dialysis (hemodialysis or peritoneal dialysis) or kidney transplantation.

A successful kidney transplant offers significant clinical and lifestyle benefits over lifelong dialysis — including longer life expectancy, freedom from dialysis machine schedules, improved dietary flexibility, and vastly enhanced overall vitality.

Preemptive Kidney Transplantation

When planned early with an eligible living family donor, a transplant can often be performed before the patient ever needs to begin dialysis (known as preemptive transplantation), resulting in optimal long-term graft survival.

Pre-Transplant Evaluation & Compatibility Assessment

A structured, multi-step workup ensures safety for both the recipient and the living donor:

  • Recipient Medical Evaluation: Thorough cardiac clearance, pulmonary function testing, infection screening, and vascular assessment of iliac blood vessels.
  • Living Donor Evaluation: Rigorous health screening to ensure the donor has two healthy kidneys, normal blood pressure, normal glucose tolerance, and excellent renal reserve. Donor safety is the apex ethical priority.
  • Immunological Compatibility: ABO blood group compatibility testing, HLA tissue typing, and CDC/Flow crossmatch testing to prevent antibody-mediated rejection.
  • Statutory Legal & Ethical Clearances: Strict adherence to India's Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act (THOTA) and state authorization committees.

The Surgical Procedure

Renal transplantation involves two coordinated surgical operations:

  • Minimally Invasive Donor Nephrectomy: Performed laparoscopically through keyhole incisions, ensuring the living donor experiences minimal discomfort, rapid mobilization, and discharge within 3–4 days.
  • Recipient Graft Implantation: The donor kidney is positioned in the recipient's lower abdomen (iliac fossa). Dr. Chipde meticulously anastomoses the renal artery and vein to the recipient's iliac vessels and connects the donor ureter to the bladder (ureteroneocystostomy).

Hospital Recovery & Post-Transplant Medicines

Following surgery, the recipient is monitored in a specialized surgical ICU with reverse barrier nursing to prevent infections:

  • Immediate Graft Function: Monitoring hourly urine output, serum creatinine normalization, and fluid balance.
  • Immunosuppressive Regimen: Daily triple-drug immunosuppressive therapy (calcineurin inhibitors, antimetabolites, and steroids) tailored to prevent organ rejection while balancing infection risks.
  • Discharge Timeline: Most recipients are safely discharged within 7 to 10 days, equipped with comprehensive medication schedules and hygiene guidance.

Long-Term Follow-Up & Graft Surveillance

Transplantation is a lifelong partnership. Dr. Chipde and the Apollo Hospitals nephrology-urology team conduct routine outpatient monitoring:

  • Frequent serum creatinine and drug-level monitoring during the initial 3 months
  • Routine blood pressure, glycemic, and lipid profile monitoring
  • Doppler ultrasound surveillance of graft renal blood flow
  • Dietary counseling, hydration maintenance, and proactive infection management
