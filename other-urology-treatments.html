Comprehensive Urological Care

Advanced tertiary-level clinical services covering Uro-Oncology, Female Urology, Bladder Incontinence, Reconstructive Procedures, and Pediatric Urology.

Full Clinical Spectrum

1. Uro-Oncology & Urological Cancers

Specialized multidisciplinary surgical management of tumors affecting the urinary and male genital tracts:

  • Prostate Cancer: PSA screening, multiparametric MRI (mpMRI), MRI-guided prostate biopsy, and robotic-assisted radical prostatectomy.
  • Kidney Tumors (Renal Cell Carcinoma): Robotic partial nephrectomy (preserving unaffected kidney tissue) or radical nephrectomy.
  • Bladder Cancer: Transurethral resection of bladder tumor (TURBT) with blue-light visualization, intravesical BCG therapy, and radical cystectomy with neo-bladder reconstruction.
  • Testicular Tumors: High inguinal orchiectomy, tumor marker tracking, and retroperitoneal lymph node dissection (RPLND).

2. Urinary Incontinence & Bladder Disorders

Urinary leakage affects both men and women and can be effectively addressed:

  • Stress Urinary Incontinence (SUI): Involuntary leakage with coughing or exercising. Treated with pelvic floor rehabilitation or mid-urethral sling procedures (TVT/TOT).
  • Overactive Bladder (OAB) & Urgency: Sudden, uncontrollable urge to urinate. Managed with bladder retraining, beta-3 adrenergic agonists, and intravesical Botox injections.
  • Neurogenic Bladder: Care for patients with spinal trauma, stroke, or Parkinson's to protect upper renal tract function.

3. Female Urology

  • Recurrent Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs): Identification of structural predispositions, post-menopausal atrophic changes, and tailored prophylactic strategies.
  • Pelvic Organ Prolapse: Surgical repair of cystocele and rectocele using native tissue or minimally invasive sacrocolpopexy.
  • Urethral Diverticulum: Surgical excision to resolve chronic dysuria and recurrent discharge.

4. Reconstructive Urology & Strictures

For urethral strictures causing urinary straining:

  • Urethroplasty: Definitive surgical reconstruction using buccal mucosal graft (BMG) from the inner cheek, providing long-term patency.
  • Pyeloplasty: Correction of pelvi-ureteric junction (PUJ) obstruction to relieve kidney hydronephrosis.

5. Pediatric Urology

  • Undescended Testis (Orchidopexy)
  • Hypospadias repair
  • Vesicoureteral Reflux (VUR) – Deflux endoscopic injection or ureteric reimplantation

Hematuria (Blood in Urine) Warning

Any episode of visible blood in the urine (gross hematuria) or persistent microscopic hematuria should never be ignored and warrants prompt investigation with ultrasound/CT and cystoscopy to exclude underlying tumors or stone disease.
