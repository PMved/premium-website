Appointment & Cancellation Policy

Apollo Rajshree Hospital OPD Practice • Dr. Saurabh Chipde

1. Consultation Scheduling Workflow

Submitting an appointment request form through this website or sending an inquiry via WhatsApp generates a scheduling coordination request. It does not represent an instant, confirmed consultation slot. Our clinical coordinator at Apollo Rajshree Hospital reviews the request, verifies doctor availability, and contacts the patient via telephone or WhatsApp message to confirm the final date, time window, and token sequence.

2. Outpatient Consultation Schedule & Venue

Clinical Venue: Apollo Rajshree Hospital, Sector-D, Scheme No. 74C, Vijay Nagar, Indore, MP 452010.
Regular OPD Hours: Monday through Saturday: 10:00 AM – 04:00 PM
Clinical Inquiries: +91 78693 92498 | WhatsApp Desk Available

3. Consultation Fee & Hospital Payment

All outpatient consultation, procedure, and diagnostic fees are established in accordance with Apollo Rajshree Hospital standard administrative schedules and are payable directly at the hospital OPD billing desk. This website does not process credit cards, debit cards, or advance online payments.

4. Rescheduling & Cancellation Notice

If you need to reschedule or cancel your consultation appointment, we request that you notify our clinic desk at least 2 to 4 hours in advance via phone call or WhatsApp message on +91 78693 92498. Timely notice enables our team to allocate the consultation window to waitlisted patients or individuals with acute symptomatic distress.

5. Emergency & Priority Triage

In accordance with hospital clinical ethics, urgent and emergency patients (e.g. patients with severe acute renal colic, post-transplant complications, or catheter emergencies) receive immediate clinical triage. On rare occasions, emergency inpatient surgeries or urgent operative procedures may cause brief adjustments to outpatient consultation start times. Our desk keeps patients informed in such circumstances.

6. Patient Pre-Consultation Checklist

To ensure a comprehensive clinical evaluation, patients are advised to bring:

  • All previous ultrasonography (USG), CT scan, or MRI films and reports.
  • Recent laboratory reports (Urine Routine & Microscopy, Serum Creatinine, Blood Urea, PSA).
  • Current medication list and discharge summaries from previous surgeries.
