Advanced Minimally Invasive Prostate Care

Over 100+ Rezūm™ procedures performed. Dr. Chipde offers a complete range of organ-sparing treatments tailored to your prostate anatomy and lifestyle priorities.

Steam Technology

Rezūm™ Water Vapor Therapy

Uses natural thermal energy delivered via convective steam to shrink obstructing prostate tissue in an outpatient setting without major surgery.

Detailed Rezūm Guide
Mechanical Lift

UroLift® System

Permanently places tiny transprostatic implants to hold open the urethra without heating, cutting, or removing prostate tissue.

Learn About UroLift
Temporary Nitinol Stent

iTind™ Procedure

A temporary nitinol basket gently reshapes the prostate channel over 5-7 days and is then completely removed, leaving no foreign implants.
