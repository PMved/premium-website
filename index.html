Featured Minimally Invasive Option

Rezūm™ Water Vapor Therapy

A minimally invasive treatment option for selected men with an enlarged prostate (BPH), utilizing the natural thermal energy stored in water vapor to relieve obstruction without traditional cutting.

Experienced Provider of Rezūm™ Therapy in Central India
01

Steam Delivered

Targeted thermal water vapor is delivered via a specialized device into obstructive prostate tissue during a short outpatient procedure.

02

Tissue Shrinks

The condensed water vapor gently disrupts excess cellular membranes. The treated tissue is gradually and naturally resorbed by the body over weeks.

03

Symptoms Improve

As the prostate channel widens, natural urinary flow and quality of sleep improve steadily, with high rates of preserved sexual function.
