Rezūm™ Water Vapor Therapy in Indore

A proven, minimally invasive daycare treatment that harnesses convective steam energy to shrink enlarged prostate tissue without cutting or foreign implants.

100+ Procedures Performed • Specialized Experience

What is Rezūm™ Water Vapor Therapy?

Rezūm™ is an innovative, FDA-cleared minimally invasive technology designed to treat lower urinary tract symptoms caused by Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH). Instead of electrosurgical tissue cutting or major incisions, Rezūm utilizes the natural thermodynamic properties of water vapor.

Dr. Saurabh Chipde is among the pioneers of this technology in Central India, having successfully performed over 100+ Rezūm procedures at Rajshree Apollo Hospitals Indore with high patient satisfaction.

How Does Steam Therapy Work?

The procedure is performed endoscopically through the natural urinary channel without any external incisions:

  • Targeted Delivery: A specialized delivery device is inserted into the urethra under direct visualization.
  • Controlled Thermal Injections: Small, precise bursts of sterile water vapor (lasting 9 seconds each) are delivered directly into the hyperplastic prostate tissue.
  • Cellular Deactivation: When the steam comes into contact with body tissue, it immediately condenses back to water, releasing stored thermal energy that deactivates the targeted prostate cells.
  • Natural Absorption: Over the following 6 to 12 weeks, the patient's natural immune process reabsorbs the deactivated tissue, shrinking the prostate and reopening the urinary channel.

Key Preservation Advantage

Unlike traditional TURP surgery, Rezūm carries an exceptionally low risk of causing erectile dysfunction or retrograde ejaculation, making it an attractive option for sexually active men.

Who May Be Suitable for Rezūm?

  • Men aged 50 and older with moderate to severe urinary symptoms caused by BPH
  • Prostate volumes typically between 30cc and 80cc (including patients with an obstructing median lobe)
  • Patients who want to stop taking daily prostate medications due to side effects or lack of efficacy
  • Men who wish to preserve sexual, erectile, and ejaculatory function
  • Patients who prefer an outpatient daycare procedure over an extensive hospital stay

What Happens During the Treatment?

Rezūm is completed in a daycare setting in approximately 10 to 15 minutes. It can be performed under local prostate block and mild oral sedation, avoiding general anesthesia risks. Most patients return home the same day.

Recovery Expectations & Post-Treatment Timeline

  • Temporary Catheter: Due to natural transient swelling in the treated area, a temporary urinary catheter is usually placed for 3 to 5 days.
  • Resuming Normal Activities: Most men return to light daily activities within a few days.
  • Progressive Improvement: Symptom improvement typically begins around week 3 to 4, with maximum flow rate improvements seen between 6 to 12 weeks as tissue is steadily reabsorbed.

Possible Risks & Side Effects

In accordance with medical safety and transparent informed consent, patients should be aware of transient side effects during the initial healing phase:

  • Mild dysuria (burning sensation during urination) for 1-2 weeks
  • Temporary increase in urinary frequency or urgency
  • Mild hematuria (traces of blood in urine)
  • Temporary need for catheter re-insertion if transient retention occurs
