Rezūm™ Water Vapor Therapy in Indore

A gentle, minimally invasive treatment option for selected men with an enlarged prostate, using natural thermal steam energy.

Performed as a short day-care procedure under local anesthesia or sedation, Rezūm relieves urinary obstruction caused by BPH without traditional surgical cutting or thermal laser vaporization, offering high rates of preserved erectile and ejaculatory function.

How It Works

The 3-Step Biological Process

Rezūm harnesses convective water vapor energy to treat enlarged prostate tissue naturally and efficiently.

01

Steam is Delivered

A fine handheld device is guided endoscopically through the urethra. Targeted, sterile water vapor is delivered in 9-second pulses directly into the obstructing prostate adenoma.

02

Targeted Tissue Shrinks

The vapor disperses through the intercellular spaces, condensing back into water and releasing gentle thermal energy that denatures targeted cell membranes without damaging surrounding nerves.

03

Symptoms Improve

Over the subsequent weeks, the body's natural healing response absorbs the treated cellular tissue. The urethral channel widens, restoring smooth, strong urinary flow.

Patient Journey

Expected Recovery Timeline

Transparency regarding recovery ensures realistic expectations and peace of mind.

Day 0

Treatment Day

The procedure takes approximately 10–15 minutes. After a short period of clinical monitoring in day-care, patients return home the same day.

Days 1–5

Early Recovery

A temporary catheter is placed while initial tissue inflammation resolves. Light activities are resumed at home while drinking adequate water.

Weeks 2–4

Initial Improvement

The catheter is removed. Patients begin noticing easier voiding, reduced straining, and fewer nocturnal wake-ups to pass urine.

Weeks 6–12

Optimal Flow

Continued natural tissue resorption leads to steady, durable urinary flow enhancement and improved overall bladder emptying.

Clinical Note on Recovery: Recovery experiences, catheter duration, and symptom improvement rates vary between individuals based on initial prostate volume, median lobe presence, and baseline bladder tone.

Potential Advantages

  • Minimally Invasive: No general incisions; performed through the natural urinary channel.
  • Sexual Function: Sexual side effects are generally less common with Rezūm than with traditional resection, preserving erectile and ejaculatory function in most men.
  • Rapid Routine Return: Most patients return to regular, sedentary activities within a few days.
  • Medication Discontinuation: Offers many men the opportunity to discontinue long-term daily BPH pills.

Candidacy & Considerations

  • Temporary Catheter: Swelling after steam application requires a urinary catheter for a few days post-procedure.
  • Gradual Relief: Unlike cutting surgery, symptom relief occurs gradually over 6 to 12 weeks as tissue shrinks.
  • Gland Volume Limits: Typically recommended for prostate volumes between 30g and 80g (though selected larger glands can be evaluated).
  • Common Transient Symptoms: Mild burning during urination or light blood in urine may occur during early recovery.
Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Rezūm in Indore

Rezūm is most commonly performed under oral sedation with local prostatic block, or light intravenous sedation. General anesthesia is rarely needed, making it suitable for older patients with cardiovascular considerations.

Clinical studies show that the large majority of patients are able to successfully discontinue alpha-blockers (such as tamsulosin or silodosin) and 5-ARIs once symptom relief stabilizes at 6 to 12 weeks.
